HumanoidBehavior is available for acquisition.

HumanoidBehavior.com — a robotics evaluation brand with a working platform, a versioned behavior library and a reproducible MuJoCo evaluation pipeline — is available for acquisition. Inquire to discuss terms and transfer scope.

What is included

  • The domain: humanoidbehavior.com — a category-defining .com for humanoid robot behavior.
  • The evaluation platform: define, version and benchmark robot behaviors, with workspaces, dashboards and an API workflow.
  • The behavior library: versioned, reusable behavior specifications including Pick & Place, Open a Door and Human Handover.
  • The MuJoCo evaluation pipeline: controlled experiments with explicit seeds and policy versions, measured runs and persisted experiment reports.
  • Documentation and demo: technical docs, a live product demo and the brand and design system.

Who this is for

Robotics teams, embodied-AI labs and humanoid robot companies that want a ready-made evaluation brand and infrastructure instead of rebuilding task definitions and benchmarking tooling from scratch.
