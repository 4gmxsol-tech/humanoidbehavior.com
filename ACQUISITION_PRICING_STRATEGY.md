# HumanoidBehavior.com — Acquisition Pricing Strategy

## Position

HumanoidBehavior.com is being offered as a strategic robotics infrastructure asset, not as a revenue-multiple SaaS business.

There is currently no live paid checkout and no recurring revenue claim. The valuation therefore rests on the combined value of:

- category-specific .com domain and brand;
- functioning robotics evaluation MVP;
- browser-based measured MuJoCo execution;
- behavior registry and versioned task specifications;
- experiment persistence and evidence workflow;
- API/developer foundation;
- acquisition time saved versus rebuilding the product foundation.

## Recommended negotiation bands

| Band | Price | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Public / marketplace anchor | $149,000 | High anchor without implying enterprise-scale traction |
| Strategic target | $75,000–$125,000 | Preferred negotiation zone |
| Strong strategic floor | $40,000–$60,000 | Consider only for a credible strategic buyer with clean terms |
| Opportunistic quick-sale floor | $25,000 | Do not advertise; use only if speed is materially more important than price |

These are seller-side asking/negotiation targets, not an independent third-party appraisal.

## Why the anchor is higher than a domain-only listing

A buyer is not being asked to purchase a parked domain. The acquisition package includes an implemented product foundation, live infrastructure, documented architecture, reproducible simulation workflow and a clear robotics-specific expansion path.

The strategic buyer may value avoided engineering time and a faster path to a dedicated robotics evaluation product more than a general website buyer would.

## Negotiation rules

1. Do not volunteer the minimum acceptable price.
2. Lead with strategic fit and the working product.
3. Ask the buyer what they want to acquire before offering concessions.
4. Prefer clean asset-sale terms over complicated earn-outs unless the buyer is exceptional.
5. If a buyer asks for exclusivity during diligence, use a short, written, time-limited exclusivity period.
6. Never transfer the domain, repository or production infrastructure before the agreed escrow/payment conditions are satisfied.
7. Any price reduction should buy something in return: faster close, cash at closing, narrower scope, limited support, or fewer warranties.
8. Treat legal, tax, escrow and transaction fees separately from the headline purchase price.

## Offer handling

### If the first offer is below $25k
Treat it as a low signal unless the buyer provides an unusually strong strategic reason. Counter or decline rather than negotiating against yourself.

### $25k–$50k
Possible acquisition range, but require a clean transaction and limited post-sale obligations.

### $50k–$100k
Serious range. Move to diligence if the buyer is credible and the asset scope is clear.

### $100k–$150k
Strong outcome for the current stage. Prioritize transaction certainty and terms.

### $150k+
Exceptional strategic outcome. Require careful legal review and a serious buyer process.

## Support boundaries

The advertised transaction should include a defined handoff period, not indefinite founder support.

Recommended baseline:

- 7–14 days of reasonable technical handoff;
- deployment walkthrough;
- architecture explanation;
- credential/account migration assistance;
- no guarantee of future development unless separately contracted.
