## Outreach qualification

Prioritize a company when at least two signals are present:

- actively developing humanoid or general-purpose robots;
- publicly discussing robot policy/model evaluation;
- building simulation infrastructure;
- hiring robotics/embodied-AI engineers;
- releasing benchmark/evaluation work;
- raising significant capital or expanding deployment;
- already operating a developer/research ecosystem.
